# Nebulaw-wallpaper
A live wallpaper for lively wallpaper app.

Transform your desktop into an ambient, high-performance visual playground.
Built using lightweight JavaScript HTML5 Canvas and CSS web technologies, Cyber Interactive Wallpaper Engine combines deep space nebula visuals, futuristic cyber-grid matrices, and smooth particle physics—all reactive to your cursor in real time.✨
Key Features:
- Interactive Cursor Modes:Magnetic Attractor: Particles smoothly orbit your cursor like miniature satellites without collapsing into a single point.Shield Repulsion: Creates a forcefield that pushes particles away as you navigate your desktop.Singularity Follower: Black hole core dynamically shifts and tracks your mouse movements across the screen.

- Click Shockwave Explosions: Left-clicking triggers color-matched energy shockwaves that pulse across your display.
- Motion Dynamics:Constellation Network: Dynamic nodes drift and bind together with glowing energy lines.
- Central Singularity: Particles spiral inward into a central accretion disk before re-emerging smoothly from off-screen borders.
- Hyperdrive Warp: 3D perspective particle trails warp past your screen for a high-speed starfield feel.

- Vibrant Color Palettes:Cyberpunk: Neon Pink & Electric CyanRGB Prism: Full SpectrumDeep Violet: Cosmic Purple & Gold AccentsEmerald Matrix: Cyberpunk GreenSolar Gold: Warm Amber & Flare OrangeIce White: Minimalist Arctic Frost

-⚙️ Performance & OptimizationZero-Edge Cluttering: Smart off-screen spawning and boundary wrapping prevent particles from stacking or getting stuck at display edges.

-Hard-Capped Particle Limits: Optimized $O(N)$ particle loops and square-distance checks ensure smooth 60 / 144 FPS without CPU spikes.Auto-Idle Mode: Smoothly drifts back to autonomous ambient motion after 3 seconds of cursor inactivity.
